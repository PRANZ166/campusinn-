## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, forward-looking collegiate ecosystem designed for students, administrators, and recruitment partners. The emotional target is high agency, optimism, and institutional trust: academic management meets a student-centric social feed.

The aesthetic fuses contemporary digital editorial styling with tactile product clarity. Expressive display headlines inject vitality into milestone achievements (such as placement offers, campus events, and guild announcements), while razor-sharp functional typography maintains executive-level clarity across grading, enterprise scheduling, and ATS-driven recruitment workflows.

Key stylistic tenets:
- **Luminous Surfaces:** Canvas spaces leverage warm off-white and porcelain tones layered with pure crystalline cards, banishing sterile academic gray.
- **Electric Precision:** Deep violet anchor points provide academic authority, paired with vivid violet pulses that denote live campus pulses, pending applications, and key milestones.
- **Tactile Generosity:** Generous target zones, soft layered depths with violet-tinted diffusion, and crisp micro-borders create an interface that feels responsive and alive.

## Colors

The system uses a chromatic hierarchy built on an electric violet core, balanced by clean porcelain backgrounds and slate ink.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#6D28D9`):** The catalytic brand engine. Used for critical CTAs, active selection states, high-priority notifications, and signature focus rings.
- **Secondary (`#EDE9FE`):** Soft lavender wash. Provides low-friction containment for interactive badges, auxiliary chips, table active rows, and conversational message bubbles.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Placement success emerald. Reserved for job offers, completed milestones, GPA honors, and affirmative validations.
- **Neutral Ink (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate primary text. Replaces pure black to eliminate harsh contrast against bright white surfaces while providing crisp legibility.
- **Supportive Neutrals:**
  - Canvas Base: `#F8FAFC` to `#FBFBFE` (warm off-white foundation).
  - Elevated Surface: `#FFFFFF` (pure white sheet).
  - Subtle Lilac Tint: `#F5F3FF` (tinted container background for featured announcements).
  - Structural Borders: `#E2E8F0` resting; `#CBD5E1` active boundaries.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Warning: `#F59E0B` (approaching deadlines, attendance alerts).
  - Informational: `#0284C7` (campus logistics, system memos).

### Dark Mode Principles
Dark modes invert canvas roles into deep charcoal (`#0B0F19`) and elevated obsidian navy (`#151C2C`, `#1E293B`). Primary violet shifts up to vibrant lilac shades (`#8B5CF6`, `#A78BFA`) to preserve 4.5:1 WCAG contrast against dark backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the expressive, spirited nature of Bricolage Grotesque with the crystalline utilitarian legibility of Hanken Grotesk.

- **Bricolage Grotesque (Headlines & Metrics):** Brings youthful energy, rhythmic idiosyncrasies, and warmth into major touchpoints. Headings feature tight negative letter-spacing for punchy impact across student dashboards, achievement banners, and placement showcases.
- **Hanken Grotesk (Body, Labels & Interactive UI):** Clean, structural, and neutral. Retains clear word shapes and sharp numeric figures across data-heavy student transcripts, job boards, schedule calendars, and institutional forms.
- **Hierarchy Guideline:** Never use Bricolage Grotesque below 18px (`headline-sm`). For buttons, chips, table headers, forms, and small UI chrome, use Hanken Grotesk with deliberate weight increases (`600` or `700`) to guarantee high-velocity scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an 8-point rhythmic grid within a flexible fluid-container model that accommodates both high-density management consoles and immersive student streams.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` (32px) gutters, and a maximum canvas container of `1440px` with `3rem` margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `1.5rem` margins. Sidebars collapse to responsive overlay drawers or floating icon rails.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1rem` safe margins. Key actions anchor to floating bottom sheets or sticky app-bars.

### Spacing Philosophy
Layout gaps prioritize generous negative space to reduce cognitive fatigue during intense exam schedules and placement crunch periods. Interior card padding scales uniformly using `space-md` for compact analytical widgets and `space-lg` for student portfolio spotlights and hero modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via luminous ambient shadows with subtle violet undertones, replacing drab neutral drops with an energetic light signature.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface color `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation. Demarcated with ultra-fine hair-thin outlines (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Feeds):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a diffused violet shadow:  
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.05);`
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards, Menus):**  
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.08);`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Placement Offer Drawers, Command Palettes):**  
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 25px 50px -12px rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.15);`

### Border Accents
Borders work in tandem with shadows. Every elevated surface uses a subtle border (`1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`) to preserve clean geometry against bright screens and varied campus lighting.

## Shapes

The design uses a rounded geometry standard (`roundedness: 2`) that brings approachable modern structure across all form factors.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Checkboxes, form inputs, secondary badges, and data tables.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px - `rounded-lg`):** Primary content cards, student resume preview tiles, analytical charts, and slide-in panels.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px - `rounded-xl`):** Highlight hero sections, modal dialogues, recruitment banners, and placement alert cards.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Status chips, student cohort tags, filter pills, and primary floating action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#6D28D9` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Hanken Grotesk` (weight 600). Border radius `0.5rem`. Hover triggers a micro-elevation shift and transition to `#5B21B6`. Focus renders an offset ring: `2px solid #FFFFFF, 2px solid #6D28D9`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#EDE9FE`, text `#6D28D9`. Hover deepens to `#DDD6FE`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#334155`, hover background `#F5F3FF` with text `#6D28D9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Placement & Status Badges:** Rendered as full pills (`9999px`).
  - Active / Shortlisted: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, tertiary border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
  - Pending Review: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
  - Campus Role / Cohort: Background `#EDE9FE`, text `#5B21B6`, border `1px solid #DDD6FE`.

### Input Fields
- **Resting:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Padding `0.75rem 1rem`, border radius `0.5rem`.
- **Focus:** Border transitions to `#6D28D9` paired with an ambient ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.15)`.
- **Validation:** Error triggers `#EF4444` border and red focus ring; success triggers `#10B981`.

### Cards & Dashboards
- **Campus Feed & Opportunity Cards:** White surface (`#FFFFFF`), border radius `1rem` (`rounded-lg`), subtle border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, ambient violet shadow. Cards with cover imagery use a 16:9 ratio with corner radius matched to internal inset limits (`0.75rem`).
- **Interactive Action Cards:** Scale up subtly (`scale(1.01)`) with increased purple ambient elevation on desktop hover.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `1.25rem` square/circular interactive footprint with `0.25rem` radius on checkboxes. Resting border `#CBD5E1`. Checked state fills `#6D28D9` displaying a crisp white icon.

### Placement & Milestone Tracker (Specialized Component)
- Horizontal/vertical stepped pipeline featuring pill connectors. Completed milestones present emerald indicators (`#10B981`), current stage pulses with violet accent glow (`#6D28D9`), and upcoming gates remain outlined slate.
