Hackathon Problem Statements

National Hackathon 2025 • MET Bhujbal Knowledge City

Track 1: AI for Education PS-AI-01

Intelligent Multimodal Doubt Clarification & Adaptive Learning System

Develop an offline-capable, multimodal tutoring agent capable of analyzing student textbook diagrams, handwriting, and voice prompts to deliver personalized step-by-step Socratic explanations without hallucinating solutions.

Objective: <1.5s latency, >92% accuracy on SPPU curriculum tests
Track 2: Smart Campus Management PS-IOT-02

Dynamic Classroom Energy & Resource Allocation via Sensor Mesh

Design an edge-computing IoT sensor platform that dynamically monitors auditorium, lab, and classroom occupancy to automate HVAC and power grids while providing instant room booking clash avoidance.

Objective: 28% verified energy reduction across campus blocks
Track 3: Sustainable Agriculture PS-AGRI-03

Early Crop Blight & Soil Moisture Telemetry for Grape Farmers

Create a mobile-first computer vision model tailored for Nashik vineyard farmers that detects fungal infections on leaves from smartphone cameras and recommends organic treatment protocols in Marathi.

Objective: On-device inference under 100ms with multilingual audio output
Track 4: Healthcare Accessibility PS-HEALTH-04

Rural Tele-Triage & Critical Drug Cold-Chain Inventory Tracker

Build a decentralized dispatch protocol linking primary health centers (PHCs) with district hospitals, tracking temperature-sensitive anti-venom and vaccine supplies with automated expiry alerts.

Objective: Zero critical stockouts and GPS-verified shipment audit log
Track 5: Environmental Monitoring PS-ENV-05

Godavari River Dissolved Oxygen & Industrial Effluent Sensor Grid

Deploy an open telemetry pipeline aggregating water turbidity, pH, and dissolved oxygen sensors along industrial riverbanks with an automated civic whistleblowing dashboard.

Objective: Public real-time API with instant civic alert thresholds
Track 6: Financial Literacy PS-FIN-06

Gamified Micro-Savings & Credit Literacy for Collegiate Youth

Interactive simulation engine that teaches students compound interest, student loan debt management, and UPI scam detection through bite-sized interactive roleplay modules.

Objective: Verified improvement in financial safety score
Track 7: Accessibility Technology PS-A11Y-07

Voice & Gesture Accessible Campus Portal for Visually Impaired Students

Create a screen-reader optimized navigation companion providing auditory beacons and haptic cues to guide visually impaired students across college hallways, classrooms, and exam halls.

Objective: Full WCAG 2.2 AAA adherence and voice-first operation
Track 8: Campus Safety PS-SAFE-08

Geofenced Campus Emergency SOS & Safe Transit Corridor Beacon

Build a zero-latency student emergency network triggering real-time campus security alerts, automated video snapshots, and safe walking route telemetry during late-night library and hostel commutes.

Objective: Sub-3 second emergency guard dispatch trigger
